Lirnyk

Rephrase selected text in any Mac app with AI.

Lirnyk now has its own website, with the latest download, a demo and what changed in each version.

Download for Mac Go to lirnyk.app

Lirnyk lives in the macOS menu bar. Select text in any app — Mail, Slack, Notes, a browser — and press a shortcut. Lirnyk sends the text to the AI provider you choose, or to a model running on your own Mac, and replaces your selection with the rewritten version. Press ⌘Z to undo it.

What it does

  • Works in any app where you can select, copy and paste text
  • Profiles, each with its own shortcut and prompt. Defaults: Friendly (⌃⌥F), Corporate (⌃⌥H) and Tech (⌃⌥O)
  • Write your own profiles: the prompt controls tone, style and language. Try a prompt on sample text right in Settings before you use it
  • Works with OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini, or fully offline with a local model through Ollama
  • Pick a model from a list of suggestions, or type any model ID. With Ollama, the list shows the models you have installed
  • Keeps your clipboard: what you copied before is restored afterwards
  • Shows progress next to the cursor; press Esc to cancel
  • Checks for new versions once a day and tells you in the menu; you can turn this off

How it works

  1. Select text and press a profile’s shortcut, for example ⌃⌥F.
  2. Lirnyk saves your clipboard, copies the selection, and sends it to the AI with the profile’s prompt as instructions.
  3. When the answer arrives, Lirnyk pastes it over your selection and puts your clipboard back. Spaces and line breaks around the selection are kept.

While it works, the menu bar icon pulses and a small bubble near the cursor shows progress. If something goes wrong — nothing selected, a network error, an answer that was cut off — Lirnyk shows the error and leaves your text untouched.

AI providers

OpenRouter
One key for hundreds of models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others. The easiest place to start.
OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini
Use your own key with the provider directly. Each provider keeps its own key and model, so you can switch back and forth.
Ollama (local)
Runs a model on your Mac, so your text never leaves it. No key and no cost. You can also point Lirnyk at Ollama on another machine in your network.

One model is used for all profiles. Small, fast models work well for rewriting; use Test in Settings to check your key and model.

Getting started

  • Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • Download Lirnyk and move it to Applications, or install it with Homebrew: brew install --cask cheyako/tap/lirnyk
  • Allow Accessibility access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Lirnyk needs it to copy and paste the selection for you
  • In Lirnyk’s Settings, pick a provider and paste its API key. OpenRouter gives you many models with one key
  • Prefer to stay offline? Install Ollama, pull a model (for example ollama pull llama3.2) and choose Ollama in Settings. No key needed

Frequently asked questions

Where does my text go?

Only the text you select when you press a shortcut is sent, together with the profile’s prompt, to the provider and model you chose. With Ollama it never leaves your Mac (or your own server). Your text never passes through a Lirnyk server, and Lirnyk has no account and no analytics. API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain. The daily update check asks lirnyk.app for the latest version and sends only the app and macOS versions; see the privacy page.

How much does it cost?

Lirnyk is free and open source. With a cloud provider, it bills you for what you use with your key at its usual rates. Local models through Ollama cost nothing.

Can it write in other languages?

Yes. The default profiles keep the language of your text. Write a profile such as “Translate to English” and it translates instead — the prompt decides.

Can I change the shortcuts?

Yes. Open Settings → Profiles, pick a profile and record a new shortcut. You can also run any profile from the menu bar menu.

Nothing happens when I press a shortcut.

Check that Lirnyk is allowed under Accessibility in System Settings and that text is selected. If Lirnyk was updated, remove it from the Accessibility list and add it again.

Where is the source code?

On GitHub: cheyako/lirnyk-app, under the GNU GPL v3. The Lirnyk name and icon are not covered by the license.

I found a bug or have a question. How do I reach you?
