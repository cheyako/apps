WickControl

Inventory & cost tracking for candle makers.

WickControl helps candle makers — hobbyists and small businesses alike — track material purchases, manage warehouse inventory, and calculate accurate production costs and pricing. All data stays on your device: there's no account, no cloud sync, and no tracking.

What it does

  • Log purchases: material, quantity, price, supplier
  • See current stock and its value at a glance
  • Create write-offs for each production batch, costed using FIFO
  • Add a markup to get an accurate final price for what you make
  • Available in English, Ukrainian, and Russian

Frequently asked questions

Is my data backed up or synced anywhere?

No. WickControl stores everything locally on your device only. Deleting the app or losing your device means losing its data, so back up your device regularly through iOS/iCloud backup if that matters to you.

What does the in-app purchase unlock?

The free version covers a limited number of materials and write-offs. A single in-app purchase removes that limit for unlimited materials and write-offs. There are no subscriptions.

I found a bug or have a question. How do I reach you?
